1. The model has 28 layers. We record data at each layer.

The model has 28 transformer layers. We record two signals at each layer. Signal 1 is the hidden state. Signal 2 is the MLP selection features. Select a layer in the list or move the slider. The chart shows the AUROC for each signal at each layer.
